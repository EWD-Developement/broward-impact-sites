06 / Direct answers

Questions homeowners should ask early.

Answers before the appointment.

What should Broward homeowners verify before choosing impact windows?

Verify the contractor’s active license and insurance, the exact product approval for each opening, who handles permits and inspections, the written installation scope, and the separate manufacturer and labor warranties. Compare itemized proposals with the same scope rather than comparing headline prices alone.

Do impact windows in Broward County need product approval?

Broward County is within Florida’s High-Velocity Hurricane Zone. The selected window or door assembly and its installation must meet the applicable code and approval requirements for the property and opening. Confirm the current Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance before ordering.
