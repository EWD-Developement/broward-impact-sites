# Concept D image notes

The five `*-ai.webp` photographs were generated specifically for this design
prototype on 2026-10-13. They depict the intended categories rather than real
Broward Impact projects and must remain labeled as concept imagery until they
are replaced with verified project photography.

- `hero-impact-home-ai.webp`: South Florida residence with impact glazing.
- `impact-window-installation-ai.webp`: residential impact-window installation.
- `impact-door-ai.webp`: hurricane-impact entry-door system.
- `impact-storefront-ai.webp`: South Florida commercial impact storefront.
- `es-impact-glass-ai.webp`: premium aluminum impact sliding-glass system; it
  is a visual category reference, not a representation of a specific ES model.

`eswindows-impact-glass.webp` is the ES Windows logo already used on the live
Broward Impact website. Source captured from:
https://www.browardimpact.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/ESWindows_impact_windows.webp

Do not identify the generated product setting as a particular ES Windows model
until the product and its current approval documentation have been verified.
