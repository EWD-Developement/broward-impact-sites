Single-hung impact windows in Broward

A single-hung impact window has a fixed top sash and an operable bottom sash, with laminated glass in a rated frame. It is the most common replacement for Broward homes and meets High-Velocity Hurricane Zone code when installed under a Florida-approved product listing and a permit.

Fit

When does a single-hung window make sense?

Single-hung units fit standard openings where you want a clean look and a simple, low-maintenance operation. They are usually the lowest-cost impact style per opening.

They are not the best pick where you want full-opening airflow (casement) or an uninterrupted view (picture).

Questions

Single-hung questions, answered

What is a single-hung impact window?

A single-hung impact window has a fixed top sash and an operable bottom sash, with laminated impact glass in a hurricane-rated frame.

When does single-hung not fit?

Choose casement or picture units when you need full-opening ventilation or large clear views. We recommend the best fit per opening at the estimate.

Is a single-hung window NOA approved?

The product and configuration we install must carry an applicable Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA for the permitted opening; we confirm this at the estimate. [[VERIFY: NOA numbers per series]]

Can I use My Safe Florida Home for single-hung windows?

Eligible opening upgrades may qualify. Rules and funding change, so check current program terms. [[VERIFY: current rules]]
