A qualified inspector records the features on the state mitigation form, and your insurer decides any credit under its filed rates. The short answer

Do impact windows lower homeowners insurance in Florida?

They can help. Florida requires insurers to offer hurricane-loss mitigation discounts for qualifying features, but the amount depends on the carrier, the policy, the home and which features are documented. Impact-rated windows and doors count when they protect the openings, and the form scores opening protection in tiers, so which openings are covered can change the result.

What does a wind-mitigation inspection record?

An authorized inspector documents several features of the home, not only windows. Opening protection is one line among roof and structural characteristics that can affect the wind portion of a premium.

03
The path

How do I get from installation to an insurance credit?

Complete the qualifying protection, keep the product and permit records, book a qualified wind-mitigation inspection, and send the signed form to your insurer. Your carrier reviews it under its approved rates.

  1. Define the opening scope

    Identify windows, doors, skylights and garage-door conditions, and decide whether to do the whole home or phase the work.

  2. Install approved protection

    Use products and installation details appropriate to the permitted project and keep the approval and closeout records.

  3. Book a qualified inspection

    An authorized professional documents the mitigation features and photographs the conditions.

  4. Submit to your carrier

    The insurer reviews the signed form and decides any available credit under its filed rates.

04
The records

Which records should I keep?

Keep the paper trail together. It protects you at inspection time, at renewal and if you ever sell the home.

Permit and final inspection

The permit number, the inspection sign-offs and the closeout documents from the building department. We handle permitting and give you the copies.

Product approval documentation

The Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance reference for each installed product and configuration. You can look approvals up on the Florida Building Commission site.

Photos before and after

Dated photos of each opening before and after installation help an inspector and your insurer see what changed.

Invoice, scope and warranty

The written scope, invoice and warranty terms. [[VERIFY: installation and manufacturer warranty terms]]

05
State program

How does My Safe Florida Home fit in?

My Safe Florida Home is a state program that, when accepting applications, may provide a free wind-mitigation inspection and matching grants for recommended hardening work: two state dollars for each homeowner dollar, up to a $10,000 state contribution. The program, not a contractor, decides eligibility, and rules and funding change.

Who decides if I qualify?

The state program decides, using its current homestead, insurance, property-type and construction-date rules. We can explain a proposed window and door scope, but we cannot approve a grant or promise reimbursement.

What is the right order?

Start with the official portal, get the inspection and recommendation, receive approval, and only then start construction. Starting early can put a grant at risk.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will impact windows definitely lower my premium?
No one can promise that. Qualifying opening protection can earn wind-mitigation credits, but your insurer applies its filed rates to your home and policy.
Do I need an inspection after installation?
A wind-mitigation inspection documents the protection on the state form so your insurer can review it. Ask your insurer or agent what they require.
Do partial upgrades count?
They can, but the form scores opening protection in tiers, so which openings are protected can change the result. We can help you decide whether to do all openings or phase the work.
How long are mitigation forms good for?
State guidance sets a general form-use period with exceptions. Confirm the current rule with your insurer or the Florida DFS guidance linked below.
Can you tell me how much I will save?
No. We can show you what the inspection records and what documentation to keep. The savings figure comes from your insurer.
Do you handle the permit?
Yes. We handle permitting and give you the closeout records you need for your insurer.
