01
The test

What does tested to hurricane force mean?

Impact products are tested with a large missile, a 9-lb 2x4, fired at the glass. They are then cycled through thousands of wind-pressure changes. The glass may crack, but the laminated layer keeps the opening sealed so the storm stays outside.

9 lb2x4 large-missile impact
1000spressure cycles after impact
DPdesign pressure rating on the product
What is laminated glass?

Two layers of glass bonded by a clear interlayer. When it breaks, the pieces stay attached to the interlayer instead of falling away. [[VERIFY: interlayer type per product]]

What is a design pressure (DP) rating?

The wind pressure the tested product is rated to resist. The rating belongs to a specific product, size and installation, so ask for it on your quote.

Does impact glass break?

It can crack on impact, which is how it absorbs the energy. The point is that the opening stays closed.

02
The code

Do I need NOA or Florida Product Approval in Broward?

Broward County is in Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so products need an applicable Florida Product Approval or a Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance for the specific configuration. We confirm that for your openings before you sign.

Beyond storms

What else do impact windows do for a home?

Laminated glass has benefits beyond wind. The size of each benefit depends on the product and glass package.

Forced-entry resistance

Laminated glass is harder to break through quickly than ordinary glass.

Sound control

Laminated glass can reduce outside noise. [[VERIFY: measured STC by product]]

UV filtering

The interlayer blocks much ultraviolet light, which helps protect furnishings. [[VERIFY: UV figure by product]]

Insurance review

Qualifying protection may support wind-mitigation credits.

05
Installation

How is it installed so the rating holds?

A tested product protects the home only when it is installed the way the approval requires. That is why installation and permitting are part of the job, not an add-on.

  1. Measure

    We measure each opening and note structure, stucco and access.

  2. Approve and order

    We match each opening to an approved product and configuration and order to size.

  3. Permit

    We handle the permit and drawings your city requires.

  4. Install and close out

    Installers anchor and seal each unit to the approval, then we complete the inspection and give you the records.

06
Timing

What should I do before hurricane season?

Start early. Measuring, ordering, permitting and installing take time, and demand rises as storms approach.

How long does the process take?

Permit and manufacturing lead times drive the schedule. We confirm a timeline at the estimate. [[VERIFY: typical lead time and install duration]]

Can I protect the most exposed openings first?

Often yes. We can price the whole home and a first phase, such as large glass doors, so you can see the difference.

What if a storm is already forecast?

Contact us right away. We cannot install after a warning is issued, but we can help you plan and prioritize. [[VERIFY: emergency-service policy]]

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do impact windows protect against hurricanes?
They are tested for large-missile impact and wind-pressure cycling. A properly approved and installed product keeps the opening sealed when the glass cracks.
Do I need permits for impact windows in Broward?
Yes. We handle the permit and give you the closeout records.
What is the difference between NOA and Florida Product Approval?
Both are ways a product is shown to meet code. We confirm which applies to your product, size and configuration.
Can impact windows replace shutters?
Yes. Properly approved and installed impact products can satisfy code without shutters.
Will impact windows stop break-ins?
Laminated glass resists forced entry and slows an attempt, but no window makes a home burglar-proof.
Are all impact windows the same?
No. Series, glass package, size and installation change the rating, which is why we check the approval for each opening.
