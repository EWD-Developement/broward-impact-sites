Proof

What does “tested to hurricane force” mean?

Impact windows pass a large-missile test: a 9-lb 2×4 fired at the glass, then thousands of wind-pressure cycles. The glass may crack, but the laminated layer holds, so the storm stays outside. The rating on every product is its design pressure (DP).

Do impact windows lower insurance premiums?

Qualifying opening protection can earn wind-mitigation credits, but savings vary by insurer and policy and are not guaranteed. A wind-mitigation inspection documents your features for the insurer.

Does Broward require NOA-approved windows?

Broward County is in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so products need Florida Product Approval or a Miami-Dade NOA. We confirm the approval for each product before the permit is filed.

How long does installation take?
