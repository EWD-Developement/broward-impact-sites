Price drivers

What changes the price of impact windows?

Opening sizes and the mix of styles set the baseline. Glass, finish, structural conditions and access then move it up or down. Two quotes with the same window count can differ widely because the scope is different.

Style and size

Single-hung, casement, sliding, picture and specialty shapes price differently.

Structure and access

Stucco, trim and framing conditions, second stories and tight access add labor.

Permit and engineering

Permit fees, drawings and inspections vary by city and scope.

Removal and disposal

Old windows, debris haul-off and cleanup should be a visible line.

Paying for it

How can I pay for it?

Several routes exist, and each has its own rules. We explain the options; we do not make the decision for you.

Financing

We can walk you through our financing options. [[VERIFY: lender names, rates and any no-money-down offer; do not publish until proven]]

PACE financing

PACE programs let eligible owners repay qualifying improvements through the property tax bill. Terms and eligibility vary, so read the agreement closely and compare it with other options.

My Safe Florida Home grants

When the state program is accepting applications, eligible homeowners may receive matching grant support for recommended hardening work: two state dollars for each homeowner dollar, up to a $10,000 state contribution. Eligibility and funding are controlled by the program.

Insurance credits and taxes

Qualifying protection may earn wind-mitigation credits from your insurer, but nothing is guaranteed. Ask your tax adviser about any tax treatment.

Comparing quotes

How do I compare two quotes fairly?

Put the same scope side by side before you look at the total.

  1. Match the scope

    Same openings, same series and glass, same finish work, same permit handling.

  2. Check the approvals

    Ask for the Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA reference for each product and configuration.

  3. Compare the warranties

    Product warranty and installation warranty are separate. Get both in writing.

  4. Compare the total last

    A lower total with a smaller scope is not a lower price.

Warning signs

What are the warning signs in a low quote?

Price is only one signal. These are the lines that most often disappear from a low number.

No approval reference

If a quote does not name the product series and its approval, you cannot check that it fits Broward's high-velocity zone.

Permit and inspection left out

Work without a permit can create problems at resale and with insurance. Ask who pulls the permit.

Finish work not mentioned

Stucco, trim and interior repairs can change the total after work starts. Ask what happens if conditions differ.

A deposit that is out of line

Ask for the payment schedule in writing and keep it tied to delivery and progress.

Frequently asked questions

How much do impact windows cost in Broward County?
It depends on opening size, product, glass, finish, structural work, access and permits. We measure first and publish ranges only from current sold-job data.
Why do two impact window quotes differ so much?
Usually the scope differs: product series, glass, installation method, permit handling, finish work and warranty.
Do you charge for the estimate?
No. The estimate is free and in your home.
Can I phase the work?
