Do impact windows lower insurance premiums?

Qualifying opening protection can earn wind-mitigation credits, but savings vary by insurer and are not guaranteed.

Does Broward require NOA-approved windows?

Broward is in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, so products need Florida Product Approval or a Miami-Dade NOA. [[VERIFY]]

What is a DP rating?

Design pressure: the wind load in pounds per square foot a product is rated to withstand.
